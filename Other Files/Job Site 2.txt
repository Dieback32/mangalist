No. 1 Userlytics
userlytics.com 
Record yourself completing tasks on sites and apps (and sometimes live interviews) to give feedback on the experience
No. 2 Testbirds
testbirds.com
Test websites and apps to spot bugs and usability problems, reporting what’s broken or confusing.
No. 3 Prolific
prolific.com
Take part in online research studies and surveys for universities and companies, getting paid per completed study.
No. 4 Respondent
respondent.io
Join paid research interviews and studies in your area of work or expertise, usually over video calls
No. 5 CareerHound
careerhound.io
Pulls hundreds of remote roles posted directly by companies, so you’ve got a lot more options to choose from. (This is a paid tool)
Bonus tool : Use RezPass.com to check your resume score and tailor your resume to every job you applied to (also a paid tool)
